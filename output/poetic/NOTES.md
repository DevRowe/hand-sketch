# A Life in Ten Lines: notes

Ten hand-sketched animations, one feeling each, one turn each.
Open `board.html` from this folder over any static server (`python3 -m http.server`); the sources are `src/scenes/poetic/`, regenerated with `npm run poetic`.

## The themes, and why these ten

Read in catalogue order the set is a life, so each piece hands on to the next:

| | piece | theme | the one turn |
|---|---|---|---|
| P01 | Wishes | wonder | a dandelion's seeds rise into the dusk and each unfolds into a star; one seed stays |
| P02 | Doorframe | memory, growing up | height marks climb and stop; after a long stillness a new mark at the bottom |
| P03 | Paper Boat | journeys, letting go | a sheet folds into a boat that the water carries out towards first light |
| P04 | The Red Thread | connection | a thread knots badly on its way between two houses, then lets go into one line |
| P05 | Every Spring | growth and change | a seed becomes a tree, then lives a whole year: blossom, leaf, letting go, snow |
| P06 | The Lighthouse | endurance | a lamp stutters in a storm, holds, and the beam finds its pace |
| P07 | Kintsugi | resilience | a bowl breaks, rests broken for a breath, and is mended in gold |
| P08 | Two Cups | loss | two cups steam; one stops |
| P09 | 52 Hertz | solitude | a whale's song crosses an empty ocean, nothing answers, it sings again |
| P10 | Small Light | hope | a flame nearly goes out, then takes, and finds the candle that was there all along |

I left out "feelings" as a theme on its own (every piece is one) and folded seasons into growth, so each subject, palette and kind of motion is different: a morph, a fold, a knot undone, a break, a light that reveals, a pen that writes, rings that travel.

## How the medium carries the feeling

- The pen is the gesture: the red thread hesitates and fusses through its knot before it quickens to the far window; the gold in Kintsugi presses hardest where the break was worst (`withPressure`); the doorframe names are written in a hand that changes a little every year (`cursive`).
- Morphing is evolution: seeds unfold into stars, branches grow out of buds, the paper folds from sheet to hat to boat, the knot eases into a line (`prepareMorph`, so the hand holds still while the shape changes).
- Loops are breath, not replays: steam, a flame, a beam, a whale's call, a year; every loop is seamless (`--seam`) and every intro lands its turn before the loop begins.
- Colour is mood, not decoration: nine mood palettes (`src/art/moods.ts`) with one feeling ink each, printed as riso plates over textured stock, with light as halftone (morning through a window, a lamp, first light on water).
- Stillness is used on purpose: the bowl stays broken for two seconds; the doorframe waits four before the new mark; the whale's silence is most of its loop.

## My picks

1. **Two Cups.** Nothing happens except that one cup stops steaming, and "I still make two" does the rest; it is the most restrained piece and the one that lands hardest.
2. **52 Hertz.** The rings keep going out across all that empty water and touching nothing but drifting motes; the loop turns the loneliness into a kind of dignity.
3. **Doorframe.** Fourteen years of pencil marks crowding to a stop, a long silence, and then one small coloured mark at the bottom next to the first.

Wishes is the hero on the board: it is the most beautiful opening, and it begins the life.
